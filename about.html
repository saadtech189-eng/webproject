Healix Prime is a trusted supplier of advanced medical equipment, delivering high-quality healthcare solutions to hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centers across Bangladesh. We are committed to supporting healthcare providers with reliable products that comply with international quality and safety standards.

Our product portfolio includes diagnostic imaging systems, hospital furniture, operation theatre equipment, and essential medical technologies sourced from globally recognized manufacturers. Each solution is carefully selected to ensure precision, performance, and long-term value.

At Healix Prime, we believe in building long-term partnerships through professional service, technical expertise, and dependable after-sales support. Guided by integrity and excellence, we aim to strengthen the healthcare infrastructure of Bangladesh and contribute to improved patient outcomes.
